# 1. The Problem

*You deployed a website. It works. Nobody can find it in Google. This chapter explains why.*

## What This Chapter Covers

This chapter looks at the gap between **shipping** a website and **being findable** on it. It's the starting point for the whole guide: before talking about crawling, indexing, or ranking, you need to understand that these are separate things, and that none of them happen automatically just because your site is live.

## Why It Matters

Most beginners treat "deploy" as the finish line. You push to production, the domain resolves, the homepage loads — done. But a search engine doesn't know your site exists just because a browser can reach it. There is no automatic handshake between your hosting provider and Google. If nobody has told Google (via a link, a sitemap, or a manual submission) that your URL exists, it may sit online, fully functional, and completely invisible in search results — sometimes for weeks.

This is the single most common source of confusion for people shipping their first site: "It's live, so why can't I find it when I search my own brand name?"

## Online vs. Discoverable vs. Indexed

These three states are easy to conflate, but they're not the same thing:

- **Online** — the server responds. Anyone with the direct URL can load the page.
- **Discoverable** — a search engine has found a path to the URL, usually through a link, a sitemap, or a manual submission.
- **Indexed** — the search engine has crawled the page, evaluated it, and stored it well enough to potentially show it in results.

A site can be online without being discoverable. A page can be discoverable and crawled without ever being indexed. Each stage depends on the one before it, but passing one doesn't guarantee the next.

## The Basic Problem SEO Solves

SEO, at its core, is about closing the gap between "this page exists" and "this page shows up for the people looking for it." That means making your site easy to discover, easy to crawl, worth indexing, and relevant enough to rank. This guide walks through that path in order, starting with the two pieces below.
